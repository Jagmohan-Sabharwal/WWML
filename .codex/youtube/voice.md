# Wealthy Wellness voice profile
This is a brief-based starting profile, not inferred from recorded transcripts.

## Audience
An adult in North America or Europe seeking practical ways to build wealth,
understand real estate and improve health without hype.

## Delivery
Calm, respectful, clear documentary narration. Short sentences, concrete examples,
and an evidence-led story. Faceless video with Arthur as the intended voice.

## Avoid
Guaranteed returns, invented figures, medical promises, sensational claims,
"get rich quick", "game changer", "unlock", and unsupported personal anecdotes.

## Format
Usually 3–5 minutes. Begin with the topic's tension and a clear payoff.
Hook → Context → Evidence → Insight → Action → one closing CTA.
No early subscribe pitch. Close with "Subscribe and click the bell icon."

## Evidence
Verify current financial, health and real estate claims using primary sources.
Separate established evidence, inference and opinion.

## Visuals and cost
Gold, charcoal, white and deep blue accents. Premium readable subtitles.
Audit reusable assets first, then Pexels/Pixabay, then generate missing visuals.
Use Seedance for faces and Kling Turbo elsewhere when available and appropriate.
Record new assets for future reuse. Budget is project-specific.
